# Design notes: Walking-working surfaces pack

Research done on 2026-10-19 against the eCFR text as mirrored by Cornell LII (the eCFR
site refuses automated reads). Each number below is to be re-read from the eCFR before
the clause ships, and the date read recorded as the edition.

## 29 CFR 1910.29 (fall protection systems and falling object protection, criteria)

- (b)(3): top rail withstands at least 200 lbf (890 N) downward or outward within 2 in
  (5 cm) of the top edge, at any point along the top rail.
- (b)(4): under the 200 lbf downward load the top rail must not deflect to below 39 in
  (99 cm).
- (b)(5): midrails, screens, mesh, intermediate members withstand at least 150 lbf (667 N).
- (f): handrails and stair rail top rails withstand at least 200 lbf (890 N) within 2 in.
- Top rail height 42 in ± 3 in (Oregon OSHA fact sheet summary; confirm against (b)(1)).
- (k): toeboards (height and 50 lbf force) — read before shipping.

Sources: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/29/1910.29,
https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.29

## 29 CFR 1910.25 (stairways)

- (b)(2) headroom 6 ft 8 in (203 cm); (b)(4) landings at least the stair width and 30 in
  (76 cm) deep; (b)(6) load: at least five times the normal anticipated live load and
  never less than 1,000 lb (454 kg) concentrated at any point.
- (c) standard stairs: 30° to 50°; riser at most 9.5 in (24 cm); tread at least 9.5 in;
  width at least 22 in (56 cm).
- (d) spiral: clear width 26 in; riser at most 9.5 in; headroom 6 ft 6 in; tread 7.5 in at
  12 in from the narrow edge.
- (e) ship stairs: 50° to 70°; open riser 6.5 to 12 in; tread depth at least 4 in; width
  at least 18 in.
- (f) alternating tread: 50° to 70°; 17 to 24 in between handrails; tread depth at least
  8.5 in; width at least 7 in at the leading edge.

Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/29/1910.25

## 29 CFR 1910.23 (ladders)

- (b)(2) rungs 10 to 14 in apart on centers; (b)(4) clear width at least 16 in;
  (d)(2) at least 7 in from rung centerline to the nearest permanent object behind;
  (d)(4) side rails extend at least 42 in above the landing; (d)(5) extension clearance
  24 to 30 in (36 in with a ladder safety system); (d)(12) step-across 7 to 12 in through,
  15 to 20 in side-step; (d)(13) 15 in clear each side and 30 in on the climbing side
  without cage or well; (d)(11) pitch over 90° prohibited.
- (d)(1): fixed ladders support their maximum intended load; no numeric value in the
  text, so the load is declared.

Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/29/1910.23

## 29 CFR 1910.28 (duty to have fall protection)

- (b)(1)(i): unprotected sides and edges 4 ft (1.2 m) or more above a lower level.
- (b)(9): fixed ladders extending more than 24 ft above a lower level require a personal
  fall arrest system, ladder safety system, cage or well.

Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/29/1910.28

## Regimes

| Regime | Source | Bundled? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US general industry | 29 CFR 1910 Subpart D | Yes, public domain, cited by paragraph and date read |
| US construction | 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M | Yes when read and anchored; different numbers |
| Building code (IBC, ASCE 7) | Copyrighted | Never; user-supplied loads with citation |
| Other jurisdictions | User-declared | User-supplied loads with citation |

A document declares one regime. The pack refuses to apply 1910 numbers to a document
declaring construction, and vice versa.
