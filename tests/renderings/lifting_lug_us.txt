PASS  padeye net tension
------------------------
    σ_t = P / ((W − d) · t)
    σ_t = 11.24 kip / ((3.150 in − 0.984 in) · 0.472 in)
    σ_t = 11.0 ksi
  where:
    P = 11.24 kip  (lifted load)
    W = 3.150 in  (lug width across the hole)
    d = 0.984 in  (pin hole diameter)
    t = 0.472 in  (lug plate thickness)
    σ_t = 11.0 ksi  (net-section tensile stress)
  safety factor 3.30 vs required minimum 2.00
  source: ASME BTH-1 §3-3

FAIL  padeye pin bearing
------------------------
    σ_p = P / (d · t)
    σ_p = 11.24 kip / (0.984 in · 0.472 in)
    σ_p = 24.2 ksi
  where:
    P = 11.24 kip  (lifted load)
    d = 0.984 in  (pin hole diameter)
    t = 0.472 in  (lug plate thickness)
    σ_p = 24.2 ksi  (pin bearing stress)
  safety factor 1.50 vs required minimum 2.00
  repair: increase thickness to 16 mm — from the lug thickness inverse (σ ∝ 1/t, so SF ∝ t)
  source: ASME BTH-1 §3-3

PASS  padeye_weld weld shear
----------------------------
    τ = P / (0.707 · w · L)
    τ = 11.24 kip / (0.707 · 0.315 in · 6.299 in)
    τ = 8.01 ksi
  where:
    P = 11.24 kip  (load carried by the weld)
    w = 0.315 in  (fillet leg size)
    L = 6.299 in  (total weld length)
    τ = 8.01 ksi  (shear stress on the effective throat)
  safety factor 5.25 vs required minimum 2.00
  source: AISC 360-16 §J2.4
