PASS  padeye net tension
------------------------
    σ_t = P / ((W − d) · t)
    σ_t = 50.0 kN / ((80.00 mm − 25.00 mm) · 12.00 mm)
    σ_t = 75.8 MPa
  where:
    P = 50.0 kN  (lifted load)
    W = 80.00 mm  (lug width across the hole)
    d = 25.00 mm  (pin hole diameter)
    t = 12.00 mm  (lug plate thickness)
    σ_t = 75.8 MPa  (net-section tensile stress)
  safety factor 3.30 vs required minimum 2.00
  source: ASME BTH-1 §3-3

FAIL  padeye pin bearing
------------------------
    σ_p = P / (d · t)
    σ_p = 50.0 kN / (25.00 mm · 12.00 mm)
    σ_p = 166.7 MPa
  where:
    P = 50.0 kN  (lifted load)
    d = 25.00 mm  (pin hole diameter)
    t = 12.00 mm  (lug plate thickness)
    σ_p = 166.7 MPa  (pin bearing stress)
  safety factor 1.50 vs required minimum 2.00
  repair: increase thickness to 16 mm — from the lug thickness inverse (σ ∝ 1/t, so SF ∝ t)
  source: ASME BTH-1 §3-3

PASS  padeye_weld weld shear
----------------------------
    τ = P / (0.707 · w · L)
    τ = 50.0 kN / (0.707 · 8.00 mm · 160.00 mm)
    τ = 55.3 MPa
  where:
    P = 50.0 kN  (load carried by the weld)
    w = 8.00 mm  (fillet leg size)
    L = 160.00 mm  (total weld length)
    τ = 55.3 MPa  (shear stress on the effective throat)
  safety factor 5.25 vs required minimum 2.00
  source: AISC 360-16 §J2.4
